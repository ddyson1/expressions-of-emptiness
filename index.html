expressionsofemptiness

by dd

the room keeps the shape of you

the way a glass keeps water gone —

curved around an absence,

still, and cold, and clear.

1

i have learned the weight of quiet.

it is not nothing.

it is everything,

held very carefully, and empty.

2

some mornings i am a doorway

with no house attached —

wind moving through

where a life should be.

3

and still i keep the light on.

not for anyone.

just to see

the shape of the dark.

4

about

poems written in the empty hours.

a notebook left open
for whoever finds it.

poetry

expressions of emptiness

Words for the space that stays
after everything else is said.

read

hollow hour

The room keeps the shape of you
the way a glass keeps water gone —
curved around an absence,
still, and cold, and clear.

I have learned the weight of quiet.
It is not nothing.
It is everything,
held very carefully, and empty.

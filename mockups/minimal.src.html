The writing, and almost nothing else.

Three quiet takes on a white page. The differences are small on purpose — typeface, alignment, and how much of the Swinger mark survives. A and C drop the gothic font from the page entirely; B keeps it as a single small wordmark. Verse is placeholder.

expressions of emptiness

the room keeps the shape of you
the way a glass keeps water gone —
curved around an absence,
still, and cold, and clear.

i have learned the weight of quiet.
it is not nothing.
it is everything,
held very carefully, and empty.

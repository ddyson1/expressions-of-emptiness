I · clinical void

Emptiness as dissociation

Cool blue-black. Verse set in a plain sans — unexpected for poetry, closer to clinical notes — floated low in a wide empty field. Restraint to the point of absence.

The printed pamphlet

IV · fragments

The split

The field divides down the seam; every word crosses from light-on-dark to dark-on-light. A direct nod to splitting — the same self, two irreconcilable halves. The risk pick.

How this works once it's live

A static site can't store messages on its own, so the interface needs a small backend behind it. Given your setup, the natural fit is Cloudflare Pages Functions writing to KV or D1 — no server to run. Because these poems are personal and the audience is strangers, I'd wire it as approve-before-publish: a message lands in a queue, you get to read it, and only then does it appear — plus rate-limiting and spam/abuse filtering. The interface above already reflects that (the "read before they appear" note, and new messages showing as awaiting review).
